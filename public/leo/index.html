The story so far

Timeline

Dec 2023

Leo comes on-chain

$LEO launches on Stacks, named for Muneeb Ali's real cat, first introduced to the world in a 2011 tweet about two kittens, Leo and Parker. It becomes one of the earliest community memecoins on the network.

Jan 2024

Airdrop tour

Rather than one big drop, $LEO seeds its holder base by airdropping straight into Stacks NFT communities: Megapont Ape Club first, then 0.5% of supply across active BNS holders, then a surprise drop for Honey Badgers, among several other community airdrops.

Jan 2024

Onto the exchanges

$LEO lists on CoinGecko within days of launch, and the last of the early community airdrops go out on January 20.

Feb 2024

MEXC Kickstarter

Leo becomes the first Dextools-listed project on Stacks and wins a MEXC Kickstarter vote. The same week, $LEO cracks CoinGecko's top 1000.

Mar 2024

A week of firsts

Featured twice on Stacks' own website, live value inside the Xverse wallet, and Muneeb joins the team on the Stacks DeFi show, all in one week.

Mar 10, 2024

The biggest airdrop in Stacks history

$LEO airdrops 5% of its supply, worth about $1.35m at the time, to STX holders, and burns another 5% alongside it.

Mar 13, 2024

Listed on CoinMarketCap

$LEO goes live on CoinMarketCap.

Apr 23, 2024

Happy birthday, Leo

Muneeb marks the day himself: "Happy birthday to Leo, the cat," the same date he first introduced Leo and Parker back in 2011.

Sep 2024

Leo Cats mint out

The 10,000-piece Leo Cats collection mints out on September 30, the first 10k PFP collection to sell out on Stacks. The "LEO Origins" mini-documentary follows weeks later.

Oct 31, 2024

Halloween Leo

A free Halloween Leo Bitcoin art mint on Gamma.

Dec 2024

12 days of Christmas

Daily games and $LEO prizes, plus the free LEO Ordinals Art Pass by @ReeceSwanepoel, making holders eligible for future artist drops.

Dec 2024

Community first

After the "Goat" token rug pull, the team reimburses 70m+ $LEO to affected holders from the team wallet. Leo holders land the 6th largest community allocation of the Pizza Pets airdrop, and the project turns one on December 29.

Feb 2025

Native Xverse swaps

Xverse adds direct STX to SIP-10 swaps in the wallet, with $LEO among the first Stacks memecoins featured.

Feb 18, 2025

First Bitcoin art drop

Leo's first Bitcoin art drop goes out free on Gamma for Art Pass holders, with artwork from Stacks OG @GPSC_STX.

Jul 2025

Meow Mondays

Weekly bounties and holder giveaways kick off through Zero Authority DAO, with 1.25m $LEO up for grabs in the first week alone.

Aug 1, 2025

Feline Fridays

A second weekly bounty series launches, with at least 25m $LEO committed to reward Stacks content creators.

Aug 2025

FPL league, year 3

The $LEO Fantasy Premier League goes live for a third straight season, with 2.5m $LEO in prizes plus bonus Leo Cats rewards.

Dec 2025

Two years on

Leo marks two years on Stacks: "what a wild ride this year has been."

Jun 2026

Leo Cats NFT momentum

Leo Cats get permanent on-chain inscriptions, top Gamma's 30-day volume leaderboard with 25k STX traded and land on Gamma's homepage.

Jul 2026

Zest swap spotlight

$LEO is featured in Zest Protocol's swap launch, celebrated with a Leo Cat NFT giveaway and an LP strategies bootcamp.

Jul 27, 2026

HODLMM pool goes live

The LEO/STX HODLMM pool on Bitflow opens to trading after a community-controlled launch, with 30m $LEO in trader rewards, capping a July where $LEO finished up 20%.

Jul 30, 2026

Stacks 4.0

Leo cheers on the Stacks 4.0 announcement with the rest of the ecosystem.

Aug 2026

Feed the Cat

feedthecat.app tracks $LEO buys and trader rewards. The campaign drives $20k in net buys and $200k+ in volume in its first 30 days.

Aug 23, 2026

No foundation, no games

The team confirms there's no "Leo foundation", less than 1% of supply sits in the team wallet, and over 20% has been given to the Stacks community since 2024.

Sep 2026

New look, LEO₿

A visual refresh and a new "cat tags" feature, both built with @boostx_app.

Sep 15, 2026

Leo Cats by the numbers

16th all-time in Stacks NFT volume, 2nd all-time in secondary sales and the most unique holders of any PFP collection on Stacks.

Oct 2026

Swap on Tenero

$LEO becomes swappable directly on Tenero, and 30m $LEO (~$3,000) in Feed the Cat rewards are distributed to traders.

Ongoing

Growing Stacks, one cat at a time!

Beyond the milestones, Leo keeps a steady drumbeat of community activity: sponsored events, ecosystem partnerships and recurring poker tournaments among them.
